The atelier

Made slowly, on purpose.

Aurum & Ivy began in a two-room workshop in Kala Ghoda with a single bench, a leather knife and a stubborn idea: that everyday objects should be worth repairing. Twelve years later we still make in batches of no more than forty, still burnish every edge by hand, and still name the person who made your piece on the card inside the box.
